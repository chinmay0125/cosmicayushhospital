Who We Are

Cosmic Ayush Hospital is dedicated to natural, drugless, and root-cause–based healing. We combine the ancient wisdom of Naturopathy, Yoga, Ayurveda, and lifestyle medicine to help the body heal itself naturally.

Our treatments focus on preventing disease, reversing lifestyle disorders, and restoring physical, mental, and emotional balance through personalized care. Every treatment plan is customized based on body constitution, disease condition, lifestyle, mental health, and long-term wellness goals.

About YCB

What is Yoga Certification Board?

The Yoga Certification Board (YCB) is an autonomous body under the Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India. It sets national standards for yoga training, assesses competencies, and awards certifications that are recognised globally.

Cosmic Ayush Hospital is an accredited YCB Examination Centre, authorised to conduct assessments and award certificates at all four levels — from beginner instructor to advanced teacher.

Certification Levels

Four Levels of YCB Certification

Progress from foundational practice to advanced instruction — each level recognised by the Government of India.

Level 1

Yoga Protocol Instructor

Foundation level for those beginning their yoga teaching journey. Covers essential asanas, pranayama, and guided class delivery.

  • Basic asana sequences
  • Pranayama fundamentals
  • Class structure & safety
  • Meditation introduction
Duration: 3 months · Written + Practical Exam
Level 2

Yoga Wellness Instructor

Intermediate level covering therapeutic applications of yoga for common health conditions and stress management.

  • Therapeutic yoga protocols
  • Anatomy & physiology
  • Yoga for chronic conditions
  • Stress & lifestyle management
Duration: 6 months · Theory + Practical + Viva
Level 3

Yoga Teacher

Advanced teaching competency with deep knowledge of yoga philosophy, therapy, and curriculum design for diverse populations.

  • Advanced asana & therapy
  • Yoga philosophy & texts
  • Curriculum design
  • Special populations teaching
Duration: 9 months · Comprehensive Assessment
Level 4

Advanced Yoga Teacher

The highest YCB certification — for master-level practitioners capable of training future yoga instructors and running accredited programmes.

  • Master teaching methodology
  • Research & documentation
  • Trainer of trainers
  • Advanced therapeutic yoga
Duration: 12 months · Full Accreditation Review
